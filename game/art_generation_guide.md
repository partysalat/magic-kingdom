# Art Generation Guide - Gisela's Last Stand

**Date:** 2025-11-15
**Tool:** Bing Image Creator (DALL-E 3)
**Style:** Isometric 3/4 top-down view, comic book art with cel-shading

---

## Successful Art Style

### Perspective
- **Isometric 3/4 view** (NOT pure overhead)
- Viewed at 45-degree angle from above
- Similar to RimWorld, Stardew Valley, Enter the Gungeon
- Works perfectly for top-down gameplay while maintaining character personality

### Visual Style
- Comic book art with thick black outlines
- Cel-shading for depth and volume
- Stylized but detailed (visible textures and features)
- Transparent backgrounds for easy integration
- Bold, saturated colors

### Theme
- Wild west + beach/ocean fusion
- Anthropomorphic sea creatures in cowboy gear
- Cowboy hats, bandanas, western clothing prominent
- Natural crustacean features maintained (shells, claws, texture)

## Generation Tips

### Consistency Rules
1. **Always include:** "comic book art style with thick black outlines and cel-shading"
2. **Always include:** "wild west beach theme" or "wild west saloon aesthetic"
3. **Always include:** "transparent background" for sprites
4. **Always include:** "isometric 3/4 top-down view" for characters/objects
5. **Reference games:** Mention "RimWorld or Stardew Valley character sprite" for consistency

### If Results Drift
- Regenerate with the exact same prompt multiple times
- Pick the best match from 4 variations
- Use the best result as a reference: "in the style of [previous generation]"

### Sizing & Integration
- Download at highest resolution available
- Scale down in game engine as needed
- Maintain aspect ratios
- Test readability at actual game scale

## Recommended Generation Approach

### Strategy 1: Start with 4-Direction (Recommended)
- Faster to generate (63 sprites vs 120)
- Still looks good in gameplay
- Can upgrade to 8-direction later if needed
- Diagonal movements use the closest cardinal direction sprite

### Strategy 2: Full 8-Direction (Higher Quality)
- Smoother character rotation
- More professional look
- 2x the generation work
- Best for final polished version

**Recommendation:** Start with 4-direction, test gameplay, upgrade to 8-direction during polish phase if desired.
